① What is Anatomy?
The term anatomy is derived from the Greek word meaning “to cut”.
It is the study of the structure and shape of the body and its parts and their relations to one another.
ApproachHow it is studied
RegionalRegion by region, e.g. thorax, upper limb, lower limb
SystemicSystem by system, e.g. cardiovascular system, respiratory system
💡 يعني إيه؟ الـ Regional = منطقة منطقة (صدر، طرف علوي، طرف سفلي)، والـ Systemic = جهاز جهاز (القلب والأوعية، التنفس…).
② Methods of Study

2. Viewing previously dissected material (jars)
3. Plastinated cadavers

2. Endoscopy(المنظار)
3. Radiological methods: X-ray, C.T, M.R.I, ultrasound

2. Plastic models, e.g. artificial skeleton
💡 يعني إيه؟ Cadavers = الدراسة على الجثث، وLiving = الدراسة على الإنسان الحي (الأشعة والمنظار والتشريح السطحي).
③ Anatomical Position
This position is used in describing the anatomy of the body.
The body is in the anatomical position when it is:
  • 1
    Standing upright with feet together.
  • 2
    Face is directed forwards.
  • 3
    Upper limbs are by the sides.
  • 4
    Palms of the hand are facing forwards.
  • 5
    Thumbs are directed laterally.
💡 يعني إيه؟ الوضع التشريحي هو الأساس لكل المصطلحات: واقف، الوجه لقدام، الذراعين جنب الجسم، الكفين لقدام، والإبهام ناحية الخارج (lateral).

🚨 Common traps
❌ Prone = lying on the back
✅ Prone = on the face. Supine = on the back.
❌ Sagittal plane divides the body into front and back
✅ That is the coronal plane. Sagittal = right and left.
❌ Horizontal plane divides the body into right and left
✅ Horizontal = upper and lower parts.
❌ Parasagittal plane divides the body into equal halves
✅ Equal halves = midsagittal (median). Parasagittal = unequal right and left sides.
❌ Median and medial mean the same
✅ Median = exactly in the middle line. Medial = nearer to the median plane.
❌ Proximal = away from the root of the limb
✅ Proximal = nearer to the root. Distal = away from it.
❌ Trendelenburg = head elevated above the feet
✅ Trendelenburg = feet elevated above the head (15–30° incline).
❌ In the anatomical position the palms face backwards
✅ Palms face forwards and thumbs point laterally.

🧠 Memory tricks

The three planes
S · C · H
Sagittal = Sides (right | left). Coronal = front | back. Horizontal = Horizon (upper | lower).
الـ Sagittal يقسم يمين وشمال، والـ Coronal قدام وورا، والـ Horizontal فوق وتحت.
Prone or Supine?
sUPine = face UP
Supine = lying on the back, face up. Prone = lying on the face.
الـ Supine فيها كلمة UP يعني الوش لفوق (على الضهر)، والـ Prone العكس.
Proximal or Distal?
Proximal = Proximity
Proximal = close to the root of the limb. Distal = distant from it.
الـ Proximal قريب من أصل الطرف (ناحية الجسم)، والـ Distal بعيد عنه.
Latin roots of the synonyms
Venter · Dorsum · Cranium · Cauda
Ventral = belly side (anterior). Dorsal = back side (posterior). Cranial = towards the head. Caudal = towards the tail (inferior).
Ventral = ناحية البطن (أمامي)، Dorsal = ناحية الضهر (خلفي)، Cranial = ناحية الراس، Caudal = ناحية تحت (ناحية العصعص، آخر العمود الفقري).

① Structure of the Skin
The skin consists of two layers: the epidermis and the dermis.
LayerFeatures
Epidermis (الطبقة الخارجية للجلد)Covered by a keratin layer making it waterproof.
Epidermal ridges are seen on the palmar aspect of hands and fingers (prints).
Dermis (الطبقة الوسطى للجلد)Consists of:
1. Collagen fibres
2. Elastic fibres
3. Blood vessels
4. Lymphatic vessels
5. Nerves
💡 يعني إيه؟ تحت الـ Dermis يوجد الـ Hypodermis (النسيج تحت الجلد) ويسمى أيضًا Subcutaneous tissue.
② Skin Appendages
  • 1
    Hairs(الشعر).
  • 2
    Nails(الأظافر): develop from the epidermis. Formed of a root, body, and free border.
  • 3
    Sebaceous glands(غدد الدهون): their ducts open in the hair follicles.
💡 يعني إيه؟ المطلوب حفظ الملحقات التلاتة وأجزاء الظفر:
  1. Hairs: الشعر
  2. Nails: الأظافر
  3. Sebaceous glands: غدد الدهون
  4. أجزاء الظفر: root هو الجذر المخفي تحت الجلد اللي الظفر بينمو منه، وbody هو الجزء المسطح اللي بتشوفه، وfree border هي الحافة الحرة اللي بتتقص
③ Applied Anatomy: Injections
InjectionDefinitionNotes
Subcutaneous (SC)A common way to introduce medications, like insulin and fertility drugs, into the hypodermis (subcutaneous tissue).Best sites: lower abdomen, gluteal region, thigh and arm, because the subcutaneous tissue is thick in these areas.
Intradermal (ID)A superficial injection of a substance into the dermis, which is located between the epidermis and the hypodermis.Relatively rare compared to other injections.

④ Functions of Bone
  • 1
    Transmits and supports the human body weight.
  • 2
    Forms the joints of the locomotor system.
  • 3
    Provides a wide surface area for muscular attachment.
  • 4
    Protects vital organs, e.g., the skull protects the brain.
  • 5
    Stores calcium and phosphorus salts.
  • 6
    Forms the blood elements in the bone marrow.
💡 يعني إيه؟ وظائف العظام الست: support وjoints وmuscle attachment وprotection وstorage (calcium and phosphorus) وblood formation.

🧠 Memory tricks

Needle angles
IM 90° › SC 45° › IV 25° › ID 10–15°
The deeper the injection, the bigger the angle. ID is the most superficial, so it has the smallest angle.
كل ما الحقنة أعمق الزاوية بتكبر: العضل 90°، تحت الجلد 45°، الوريد 25°، داخل الطبقة الوسطى للجلد 10–15°.
Layers of the skin

⑦ Circulatory System
There are 3 types of circulation:
CirculationPath
SystemicBegins in the left ventricle → aorta and its branches → all tissues (exchange of gases and materials) → small veins → large veins → superior and inferior vena cava → right atrium → right ventricle, where it ends
PulmonaryStarts from the right ventricle → pulmonary artery and its 2 branches → both lungs (exchange of gases) → 4 pulmonary veins → left atrium → left ventricle, where it ends
PortalVenous blood from stomach, spleen, pancreas and intestine → portal vein → enters the liver → many branches ending in liver sinusoids → hepatic veins → inferior vena cava → right atrium
💡 يعني إيه؟ الدورات التلاتة:
